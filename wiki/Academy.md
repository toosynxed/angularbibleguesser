# Academy

A Quest Mode activity focused on learning a passage, then answering questions.

## How to start

[[Quest-Mode|Quest Map]] → **Academy of Challenges**

## How it works

1. Pick a curated lesson set (examples include topics like Exodus, Genesis, or the Beatitudes)
2. Watch the embedded lesson video (or skip / continue when ready)
3. Play **5 multiple-choice** rounds about the content
4. View your local results

This is **not** the typed book/chapter/verse guess used in Normal Mode.

## Scoring

* Correct: **50–100** points based on answer speed (full **100** if untimed)
* Wrong: **0**
* Maximum = rounds × 100

Academy shows your Scroll balance in the UI but does **not** currently award Scrolls for completion.
