# Chatbot

A floating **Better Bible Guesser** assistant available on every page.

## What it’s for

* How to play and navigate the app
* Short help with modes, sharing, and features
* It is instructed **not** to spoil Bible verse answers

## How to use

1. Tap the floating chat button
2. Read the greeting
3. Ask a short question about the game
4. Get a reply from the assistant (powered by the game’s chat backend)

If the connection fails, you’ll see an error — try again in a moment.

## Tips

* Ask “How do I join multiplayer?” rather than “Where is John 3:16?”
* Keep questions about the **game**, not homework spoilers
