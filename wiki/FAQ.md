# FAQ

## Do I need an account?

No — guests can play. Sign in to save stats, daily streaks, Scrolls, cosmetics, and a stable multiplayer name.

## How do points and stars work?

See [[Scoring]]. Short version: closer verse guesses = more points (0–100). Matching book / chapter / verse earns up to 3 stars.

## What are Scrolls?

Quest currency. Earn them on the [[Racetrack]], spend them in the [[Marketplace]].

## Why can’t I play Castle?

Castle of Champions on the Quest map is still **under development**.

## Can I replay a friend’s game?

Yes — use their short code or permanent link, or open a set from the [[Verse-Board]]. See [[Custom-Create-and-Codes]].

## What’s the difference between Custom and Create?

* **Custom** — random verses from books you pick
* **Create** — you choose each verse yourself, then share or save

## Is Daily the same for everyone?

Yes — everyone gets the same five verses that calendar day.

## Where are the rules in the app?

Home → **?** Help → **Rules**. This wiki expands on those with mode and Quest detail.

## Who do I contact?

Check the app footer for the support email and Discord link.
