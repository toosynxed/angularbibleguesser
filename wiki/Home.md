## Quick facts

* Each typed-guess round scores **0–100** points and up to **3 stars**
* **Scrolls** are the Quest currency — earn them on the Racetrack, spend them in the Marketplace
* Sign in to save stats, streaks, cosmetics, and multiplayer progress
