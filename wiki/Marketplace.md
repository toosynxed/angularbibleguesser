# Marketplace

Spend **Scrolls** on cosmetics for your display name in lobbies, leaderboards, and profile previews.

## How to open

[[Quest-Mode|Quest Map]] → **Marketplace**

## Daily shop

* Each signed-in player gets **5 item slots** per day
* Slots are chosen from categories that still have unowned items
* Already owned items stay visible but greyed out as **Owned**
* If nothing is left to offer in a category path, you may see “No Items Available For Purchase!”

## Buying

1. Check your Scroll balance (Quest header / Marketplace)
2. Tap an affordable unowned item
3. On success, Scrolls are deducted and the cosmetic unlocks for [[Profile-Customisation]]
4. If you don’t have enough Scrolls, the purchase fails

Earn Scrolls primarily from [[Racetrack]].
