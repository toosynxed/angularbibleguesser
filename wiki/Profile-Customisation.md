# Profile Customisation

Personalise how your name looks in the game.

## How to open

Home → **Profile** (signed in) → **Customize**

## Options

| Slot | Choices |
|---|---|
| Name colour | Colour picker (disabled while Rainbow effect is equipped) |
| Name effect | None, Rainbow, Glow, Bold, Italic, Underline |
| Icon | None, or unlocked emoji icons (crown, fire, star, cross, dove, swords, shield, trophy, medal, …) |
| Nameplate | None, Gold, Animated Fire, Ice |

Locked cosmetics must be purchased in the [[Marketplace]] before they appear as usable options. The profile page shows a live preview of your decorated name.

## Account basics

From login / profile you can also:

* Play as a guest
* Sign in with Google or email/password
* Change display name (max **15** characters)
* Sign out or delete your account

Sign in is recommended if you want stats, streaks, Scrolls, and cosmetics to persist.
