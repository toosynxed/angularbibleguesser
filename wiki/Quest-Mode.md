# Quest Mode

Quest Mode is a map hub for extra challenges, lessons, and the cosmetics shop.

## How to open

Home → ***NEW* Quest Mode**

Your **Scroll** balance appears in the quest header.

## Map locations

| Location | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Marketplace** | Spend Scrolls on profile cosmetics → [[Marketplace]] |
| **Academy of Challenges** | Video lesson + multiple-choice quiz → [[Academy]] |
| **Racetrack** | Daily Easy / Medium / Hard timed verse sets → [[Racetrack]] |
| **Castle of Champions** | Not playable yet (under development) |

Map tooltips may mention challenge-style unlock flavour text; locations that are implemented open when you click them.

## Scrolls

**Scrolls** are Quest currency:

* Earn them by finishing [[Racetrack]] sets (signed-in)
* Spend them in the [[Marketplace]]
* New accounts start at **0**
