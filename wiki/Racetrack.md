# Racetrack

Daily timed verse sets on the Quest map. This is the main way to earn **Scrolls**.

## How to start

[[Quest-Mode|Quest Map]] → **Racetrack**

## Difficulties

Each day offers Easy, Medium, and Hard sets (**5 verses** each):

| Difficulty | Base Scroll reward | Time limit |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 20 | 120 seconds |
| Medium | 35 | 90 seconds |
| Hard | 50 | 60 seconds |

Gameplay uses the standard typed-guess UI and [[Scoring|score + stars]].

## Scroll rewards

After you finish a set while signed in, a Scroll reward is calculated from:

* Your average score
* Your average stars
* How much time you had left

Roughly: higher score, more stars, and faster clears → more of the base reward. You can earn **one reward per set per day** per account.

## Tips

* Sign in before playing if you want Scrolls saved to your profile
* Spend Scrolls in the [[Marketplace]]
* Hard mode is short on time — practise book recognition in [[Normal-Mode]] first
